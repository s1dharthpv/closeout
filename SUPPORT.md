# Support

Closeout is a free, open-source app maintained by one person, so support is best effort.

## Get help

- **Bugs and questions:** open an issue at https://github.com/s1dharthpv/closeout/issues
- **Feature requests:** open an issue with the label "enhancement" or start the title with "Feature:".

## Before you open an issue

Please include:

- Frappe and ERPNext versions (for example v16.35)
- Closeout version or commit
- Which report, and the filters you used
- What you expected and what you saw
- Whether the Trial Balance for the same filters in standard ERPNext balances

Do not paste real company names, account balances or customer data into a public issue. Use a screenshot with the numbers hidden, or a small made-up example.

## What is supported

- Frappe and ERPNext v16
- The two reports: Closeout Trial Balance and Close Readiness

## What is not supported

- Earlier Frappe versions
- Fixing data problems in your ledger, or accounting and audit advice. The reports point out what to check, and the decision stays with you and your accountant.

## Response time

Issues are usually answered within a week. There is no guaranteed response time or service level.
