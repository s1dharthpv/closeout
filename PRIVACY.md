# Privacy

Closeout runs entirely inside your own Frappe site.

## What it does with data

- It reads existing records on your site (GL Entry, Account, Fiscal Year, Period Closing Voucher, draft documents, Bank Transaction and Payment Entry) to calculate its two reports.
- It does not create, change or delete any record.
- It adds no DocTypes and stores nothing of its own.

## What it does not do

- It makes no network requests and sends no data anywhere.
- It has no analytics, tracking, telemetry or advertising.
- It does not collect personal information, and it does not use cookies of its own.

## Where your data lives

Everything stays on your site and your hosting, the same as the rest of ERPNext. If you host on Frappe Cloud or another provider, their privacy terms apply to your site, not to this app.
